## Why this video

Three hours and twenty minutes on Wrap3D: the deepest single-technique source in the whole curriculum. Wrapping — projecting a clean-topology body onto a target scan/model — is the mechanical heart of model conversion: our factory work, mature-variant production, and every "same character, new body" problem runs on it. This is the category's flagship study after the founding set.

## SHOULD-BE-SEEN (written before viewing)

A full Wrap3D workflow: source and target preparation, point/correspondence placement, the wrap itself, projection cleanup, and Blender finishing — at working length, with the failures left in.

## Timed observation focus

- Preparation contracts: what both meshes must satisfy before wrapping (topology, scale, pose, open edges).
- Correspondence strategy: how many points, where, in what order.
- Wrap parameters, numerically, and each re-wrap's changed variable.
- Cleanup: where the wrap lies (hands, face, joints) and the hand fixes, in order.
- The Blender finishing pass after the wrap.
