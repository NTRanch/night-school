## Why this video

The 2D side of the R6 problem. A working illustrator recreates ZZZ's
three illustration tiers and states the rules the estate's paint-first
face and toon stack are built on: hard-edged shadow shapes only, depth
from stacked shapes rather than blended tones, and the cool back rim
as house signature. Two of its rules were adopted outright as R6 paint
rules; it also corroborates the face kit's priority — at the close-up
tier, expression is what carries the piece.
