## Why this video

POV is a camera discipline: the body that owns the camera must exist in the scene — head motion, hand presence, eyeline, and the discipline of never showing what the viewpoint character cannot see. The Bonnie series (Plans 15, 18, 19, 20) is one creator's sustained study of a single act from several angles; this one isolates the camera problem.

## SHOULD-BE-SEEN (written before viewing)

A long-form tutorial of a POV scene build: viewpoint rig, head-bob/body-presence method, partner animation relative to a moving camera, and the render/review loop.
