## Why this video

The first source in the set to stage **two inductions of two
different transition types back-to-back on two subjects in one
work** — a controlled comparison no single-arc source can give. One
subject consolidates through a lid ladder with *no iris event at
all* (the tracked variable is aperture + focus, leaving the pattern
channel free for the peak); the other takes a flash-masked pattern
swap whose anticipation was pre-paid by a full-frame stimulus
insert, her spiral irises the stimulus graphic copied 1:1. Around
the inductions: an occlusion relay handoff between subjects, a
body-first introduction, a redundantly-encoded peak tally, and a
settle carried by a hand-hold with the state persisting. It also
carries the set's one clean negative finding: at this piece's
≈1.0 s cycle class there is **no gaze phase-lock** — the device is a
device, not a law of the grammar.
