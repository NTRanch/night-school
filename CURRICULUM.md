the founding set, named by the Owner. Plans 08 onward are the remainder
of the rule34video Tutorials category intake, added 2026-11-05 (both
category pages; the full shelf of 41, verified against the category

six plans covering seven Owner-supplied YouTube videos — SFW pipeline
study, ZZZ/Genshin-focused (character setup, cinematics analysis, art
study, extraction and retarget). The 2024 Cynological tutorial is

deltas are recorded in Plan 42's technique sheet. These plans were
authored alongside their first study run (fathom extraction,
2026-11-24) and stand at Pass 2, with their adoptions already landed
as inputs to the Prima canonical-base programme.

**Third shelf — the favourites performance studies (Plans 48–57).**
Added 2026-11-25: ten plans from the Owner's rule34video favourites
extraction set (dossiers of record in the favourites extraction
audit, 2026-11-24/25), plus one Owner-supplied Danbooru emulation
target (Plan 57) grouped with them. These are performance and
animation references — studied performances, not tutorials — and most
feed one programme: Prima's corruption-sim scene grammar and the
shared `iris_state` machine. A screened wider set exists behind this
shelf; items the fail-closed screen excluded are not in the
curriculum, are not planned, and are not named here. Plan 56 carried
a standing flag in its own record: Owner-directed intake (D-1398),
never fail-closed screened — ~~the flag is part of the plan, and its
Pass 3 is sequenced after the Owner resolves it~~. The Owner
resolved it on 2026-11-25 (decision walk #18): the item was
screened under the fail-closed standard and barred
(academy-student cast; no established adult age), and its Pass 3
is closed as never-to-run. The verdict of record is in the plan
file; Plan 56 is named in the shelf table below with its barred
marking, as the barred Tutorials plans are named in theirs.

Execution order is by dependency, not number: Plan 07 before Plan 06
(simulation before integration), Plan 01 early (it feeds the two-body
programme), the rest as capacity allows. Every plan runs the same five
passes — see `protocol/STUDY-PROTOCOL.md`.
