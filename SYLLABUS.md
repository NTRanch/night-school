# Syllabus

The course over the library.

[`CURRICULUM.md`](CURRICULUM.md) is the library: 57 study plans on
three shelves, each plan a five-pass study of one published tutorial
or performance. The library keeps its plan numbers — a plan number is
a citation, and citations do not move. This syllabus is the *path*
through the library: the plans re-sequenced into lessons that build
on one another, from a first still to a finished scene, each lesson
taught by one of the Torogao girls.

The gradient is deliberate: stills before motion, solo before
two-body, motion before effects, technique before performance,
performance before production. A student who follows the lesson
numbers in order never meets a technique whose prerequisites they
have not already gated.

## How a lesson works

Every lesson has the same anatomy:

- **The reading** — the cited plan, run under the five-pass protocol
  ([`protocol/STUDY-PROTOCOL.md`](protocol/STUDY-PROTOCOL.md)):
  intake, timed observation, distillation, clean-room rebuild,
  application and gate.
- **The lecture** — the teacher's demonstration scene: the technique
  performed on her own rig and frozen as a scene spec. Shipped
  animation in this estate is code — a frozen, hashed spec rendered
  deterministically — and a lecture is exactly that: a spec the
  student can open, read, and re-render.
- **The exercise** — the student reproduces the technique on a
  *different* girl. Reproducing it on the teacher's own rig would
  only prove copying; transfer to a second body is the evidence the
  technique was learned.
- **The gate** — the cited plan's own Pass 4/5 criteria, judged
  expected-vs-seen. Numbers support the verdict; they never carry
  it alone.

## Faculty

- **Prima — Headmistress.** Orientation (with Sloane) and every
  capstone. She is the canonical base: the rig every other girl's
  template derives from, and the body the capstone is built on. She
  teaches the two-body fundamentals herself — Plan 01 feeds the
  two-body programme, and the programme is hers.
- **Yui — Foundations & the Still Image.** Animation fundamentals,
  posing, the posed still, light and colour. The on-ramp
  department: a student's first finished work is a still, and it is
  made in her classes.
- **Alma — The Body.** Clothing, soft props, conversions, and the
  fluid simulation chain. She is the fleet's body reference
  implementation; where a technique makes a body usable, clothes
  it, or finishes its effects, she teaches it. Module 3's
  soft-body strand is Maren's, under the Owner's ruling below.
- **Sloane — The Pipeline.** Extraction, intake hygiene, census,
  retarget, conversion. The technical track: how material enters
  the estate, how it is counted, and how it is made ours.
- **Piper — Camera & Performance.** The camera as a participant:
  POV craft, duration, ending beats, editorial form. (Her classroom
  lessons sit inside Modules 4 and 9, where camera and editing
  belong; her named electives are below.)
- **Lux — Expression & Eye State.** The performance line: reaction
  grammar and the eye-state machine the favourites shelf was
  extracted to build. Her department is the face as an instrument.
- **Lana — Toys & Interactive Systems.** The most hands-on
  department in the school, taught by the cast's
  demonstrator-in-chief: she also fronts the school's demo work,
  both routes — the 3D performance route and the 2D puppet route,
  one reaction engine driving both. Her department has three
  strands: animating toys, programming toys, and the build — a
  small game the student ships as the module's gate.
- **Maren — Soft Body & the Mobile Build.** Breast and ass
  animation, jiggle physics, and lactation in Module 3's soft-body
  strand; building games for mobile in Module 8. Her home cast is
  Strobe, and she teaches at Night School by the Owner's ruling.
  Owner ruling 2026-11-25: Maren is seated as faculty; this

## Module 7 — Toys & Interactive Systems (Lana)

Placed after Performance and before Production for a stated
reason: toy programming needs the whole animation spine beneath
it — a toy in a scene is a performer, and its programming is
choreography with a safety contract — and this module's project
is the course's first full build, the bridge from lessons to the
capstone. The module is project-based: across its lessons the
student builds a small game that integrates ache-driven toys, and
the module's gate is that game, working.

The department's second strand teaches the ache MCP as the
platform: its 11-tool surface, and its safety contract taught *as*
curriculum, not as a footnote — master off by default, ceiling
clamped, synchronous stop, no resume on reconnect, the 5-second
heartbeat watchdog, and the pure decide() gate every command
passes through. Live-device work exists only at the final stage
(S4) of the S1–S4 staging, under the Owner's explicit go and
presence; every earlier lesson is spec- and simulation-level.

| 7.2 | Lana | Animating toys: toy and prop motion as first-class scene-spec content (supporting citation: Plan 24, cross-referenced from the electives) | 7.1 | A scene in which the toy is choreographed as a performer — its motion specified, frozen, and double-rendered like any actor's |
