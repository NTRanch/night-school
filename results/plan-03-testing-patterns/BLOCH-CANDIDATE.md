## Lesson (loop grammar)

A short loop presented far beyond its cycle count is held by
an **event cadence outside the loop**, not by variation
inside it. Evidence from the Plan 03 record: one driven cycle
(63–150 frames by regime) ran 82.7 s as a watchable test reel
because the operator imposed 7 events at ~12 s spacing — a
3.9 s full stop at the back extreme (f213–446), a depth
collapse-and-restore, a bias end-to-end sweep, a speed-floor
test, a speed raise for the finish. Within a regime, cycle-
corresponding frames were never pixel-equal (0 of all sampled
pairs at MAD < 0.1) yet that micro-variation is not what a
viewer reads as variety — the regime changes are. The
estate's Plan 01 loop is the complementary datum: a fixed
78-sample seam-exact cycle whose interest is authored
*inside* the cycle (leader/follower phase offsets). Two
records, two strategies — outside cadence vs inside phase
structure — and they compose. Portable rule, stated as an
observation: budget an outside-the-loop event every ~8–25 s
for any loop shown longer than ~10 cycles, or author the
phase structure that substitutes for it.

## Why this earns filing

The estate's loop work (Plan 01, the D-1 demonstrator,
Strobe/Torogao scene direction) is entirely fixed-clip;
this is the first measured record in the programme of the
parametric-sweep alternative, with frame-difference numbers
behind the cadence claim rather than an impression.
