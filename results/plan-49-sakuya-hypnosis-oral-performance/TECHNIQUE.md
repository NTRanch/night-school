## Headline devices

1. **Eye state — a three-layer parameter.** Against Plan 48's
   hue-ramp signifier, this source runs three independent layers,
   and a Torogao iris spec should too:
   - **Emissive hue** — violet (≈280°) → flare white-pink → magenta
     (≈320°). The flare is a ≤0.25 s overshoot, not a step on the
     ramp.
   - **Emissive intensity** — with the caution: this model's
     baseline eyes *already* glow. The before/after contrast is
     carried by hue + pattern, not glow on/off. A Prima spec
     starting from a natural (non-emissive) iris gets the intensity
     ramp back as a free layer.
   - **Pattern swap** — at trance lock the iris texture itself
     changes to a concentric spiral. The source separates *trigger*
     (light), *transition* (hue/consolidation), and *lock*
     (pattern) into three distinct timed events, the lock landing
     ~8 s after the flash. **Transfers as-is** as a state machine;
     the spiral itself is one pattern choice among several
     (Prima's canon heart-pupil signifier is the same slot,
     estate-owned).
2. **Gaze is phase-locked to the stroke cycle.** During the
   performance, eye contact is not continuous — it is **timed to
   the top of each stroke**, face lifting and spiral eyes opening
   into the lens at phase 0, hair covering the face at the bottom.
   A scene spec expresses this as `gaze_target = camera` gated by
   stroke phase, with the lid/hair occlusion doing the rest.
   **Transfers as-is.** (Second independent confirmation: Plan 51.)
3. **Stroke-cycle tempo map.** Full cycle ≈ **2.0 s (≈0.5 Hz)**,
   descent slightly faster than the rise; deep phase compresses
   amplitude and lengthens bottom dwell (2–4 s holds) at a similar
   period; finish stretches the period to ≈2.5 s with shallow
   strokes. The *dwell distribution* changes, not the period.
4. **Tremor (procedural layer).** A fine head/shoulder tremor runs
   in the deepening window only (amplitude ≈0.5–1° at the head;
   frequency not resolvable from the evidence — spec as a
   procedural layer, start ≈6 Hz, tune by eye) and is **off** once
   the stroke cycle starts: the source's trance is steady, not
   shaking. Flagged estimate, not a measurement.
5. **Hand-brace behaviour.** Kneeling, torso pitched ≈30–40°
   forward, both hands planted flat on the partner's thighs,
   fingers spread — the brace the head-stroke pivots against;
   fingers flex at the deepest strokes; hands flatten and the grip
   releases in the finish. Hair secondary lag ≈0.1–0.2 s.

## Register flags

- **Transfers as-is:** the three-layer eye state machine; the
  gaze/stroke phase-lock; the pant cycle (aperture ≈1 Hz, small);
  the stroke timing and its three-phase tempo map; the hand-brace
  behaviour; hair secondary motion; the freeze beat's *shape*
  (arrested attention — body still while the face changes).
- **Register translations:** the **trigger** — in the source the app
  is fired *at* her; the willing version's staging seed already
  exists in the source at t16–17.5, where she leans in toward the
  phone, curious and dismissive. Re-author the flash as the moment
  her looking becomes fixation because she does not look away;
  poses and timing transfer, the compulsion semantics do not. The
  performance phase itself contains no reluctance beats — it is
  uniformly eager — so its mechanics transfer cleanly; what changes
  is only the stated reason, carried in scene framing and dialogue,
  not in the keys.
- **Does not transfer:** the app/phone as a control device over
  her; the status-card fiction ("Hypnosis Success Rate 99.7%") —
  the state's legibility is carried by the eye state itself, which
  this source proves is readable even at full blur.
