the character is built using standard godot nodes skeleton 2d along with a few plugins
the first one is soup iK it allows you to create higher quality iK and
wiggle animations though i modified it to better fit my needs
the nsfw parts are just sprites layered on top of the main character
they open and close using simple animations and shaders
the hair is driven by a simple shader that deforms polygons creating a soft wind and swaying effect
the eyes and eyebrows are also controlled and adjusted through shaders
overall the character is made up of separate parts arms legs head and body
all deformed by bones which together create a cohesive and visually appealing result
all bones are controlled procedurally via scripts
the second plugin is poly rig it's a paid plugin i bought it for six dollars
It allows you to create a high-quality mesh
