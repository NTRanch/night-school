## 1. Sprite-to-3D factory (lumen factory; sprite → canonical rigged base)

**Which recorded failure class traces to a skipped
precondition:** the factory's conversions live or die on
exactly W-2 (pre-alignment into canonical rig space) and
W-5 (correspondence authored against the wrong geometry
state). The rebuild priced both: a 10 % scale miss costs
45–51 mm at the bust anchors (E1); a stale geometry state
passes silently at global mean 0.46 mm while its bust
anchors sit 15–18 mm off (E4). Any factory conversion
that "looked fine overall" but failed identity checks is
the E4 signature.

**Proposal (intake gate):** the factory's intake adopts
the validated checklist as a gate form — W-1 (interchange
contract named), W-2 (alignment verified numerically
against the canonical frame, not eyeballed), W-3 (named
sets only), W-5 (every correspondence/anchor set carries
the hash of the geometry it was derived from; mismatch
refuses, per the fleet's own anchor-derivation rule),
W-10 (results land as blends/keys on the canonical body,
never as mesh swaps). W-4/W-7 enter in their CONDITIONAL
forms (cavity-bearing sources; large-displacement
conversions).

## 2. Body-variant production (SIX-GIRL-STANDARD §4 bust parameter)

**The rebuild IS this application** (Prima m 1.00 →
m 1.50), and its most useful output is a negative: **E7 —
a single nearest-surface projection beat the full
contract stack on a gentle single-parameter morph**
(bust mean 0.759 vs 1.148 mm). Variant production at
Δamp ≤ 15 mm should therefore use the simple projection
+ anchor gate, and reserve the full pipeline for
cross-identity work. Two standing cautions priced by the
rebuild: (a) the apex error at production cage density
is ~7–9 mm regardless of method (cage floor — if a
variant gate ever demands < 5 mm at the nipple anchors,
the cage must densify in the bust band first); (b) never
blanket-smooth a convex feature as "cleanup"
(polish-decomp.json: +1.94 mm bust mean from one Smooth
pass).

**Proposal:** variant gates cite the anchor set (nipples,
chest) rather than global means — E3 showed a global
mean can look fine (0.444) while the pinned landmarks
sit at the full parameter gap (14.43 mm).

## 3. Headhack practice (cf. Plan 28, same creator, same Feng project)

Plan 28's run (read-only cross-reference; its files were
not touched) studies the headhack side of this exact
project; Plan 38 supplies the headhack's missing middle
act: the head was conformed by wrap BEFORE the Blender
headhack finishing that Plan 28 observes. The transferable
headhack rules from this plan: (a) correspondence is
cleared and re-authored per target — never inherited
across projects (the 127-point near-miss, W-5); (b) the
raw conform is never edited — a Correction layer carries
all hand work (W-8), which is precisely the discipline
that makes a headhack auditable; (c) lie zones are
priced in advance (ears inherent, mouth interior
inherent, sockets partly pre-emptable, neck seam
placement pre-emptable) — a headhack plan can budget
cleanup time from the map instead of discovering it.

**Proposal:** headhack intake gains two checklist lines —
"target cavity inventory" (which of teeth/mouth
interior/eye parts lack counterparts → the W-4
conditional trigger) and "correction-layer name reserved
before first edit" (W-8).
