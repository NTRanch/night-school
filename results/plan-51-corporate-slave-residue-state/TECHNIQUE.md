# Plan 51 — Pass 2 Technique Sheet (Distillation)

Source: rule34video 18215545, "The Corporate-Slave Me and
Sakurako-chan: Happily Ever After" (ettole), 260.0 s. Study run:
2026-11-25, favourites follow-on wave; dossier of record in the
extraction audit. Screen: fail-closed frame screen PASS (no
character tags existed; eight frames read before extraction — adult
throughout, full arc in scope). One location, four acts separated
by black cuts; every position change happens inside a black cut,
never on camera. Reference only (Tier A); nothing ships.

## The central fact

**There is no induction to extract.** The piece opens inside the
residue state (she is found kneeling in the entryway, eyes already
pink), and at ≈t49 a red monospace status readout states the thesis
outright: *"The target's hypnosis has already been released.
However, affection and attachment levels have exceeded their
maximum and are being maintained."* The remaining four minutes are
the evidence for that sentence.

## Grammar yield

1. **The residue state as the performed subject — TRANSFER AS-IS
   (the headline).** The set's prior hypnosis dossiers all extract
   *transitions*; this piece performs neither transition and plays
   the persistence itself for 260 s: signifier on, behaviour eager,
   release already behind her. The worked example for Prima's
   kept-softness / afterglow-floor specs: a state that does not
   decay back to baseline because the character *keeps* it. Under
   the estate inversion the transfer is direct — in Prima's
   grammar the residue is desire she owns, so the source's one
   fiction problem (the residue was installed by someone else) does
   not survive the move and does not need to.
2. **`iris_state` steady-register mode — REGISTER TRANSLATION.**
   The shared spec had three transition types (ramp /
   consolidation / occlusion_reveal). This piece demonstrates a
   fourth mode: **no transition at all** — the state is a
   scene-level constant keyed to register (service vs domestic),
   switched only at scene boundaries and bundled with costume and
   light. Spec form: `iris_state = steady(register)`. Her eyes are
   ON (magenta, emissive, star highlight, half-lid resting) for the
   whole service register and OFF (natural dark violet, soft) only
   in the epilogue; at the closest framings the pupil highlight
   reads heart-shaped.
3. **Diegetic status readout as exposition —
   TRANSLATION-WITH-ADDITION.** One line of in-fiction UI carries
   the entire causal history (released / max / maintained). The
   *mechanism* — a display layer stating the state machine's
   values — transfers to Prima's display programme; the *voice*
   does not: her readout must be hers (her own account of her
   state, in the Ask register), and it can be wrong, coy, or
   partial — hers to phrase — which the source's omniscient readout
   never is.
4. **Gaze phase-lock at cycle top — TRANSFER AS-IS.** In the
   cowgirl cycle her face lifts and her eyes open to the camera at
   the **top** of each ≈2.0 s bounce; at the bottom her head drops,
   hair falls forward, gaze leaves. Second independent confirmation
   of Plan 49's device — same period class, same phase: promote
   from dossier finding to standing grammar.
5. **Performing into the recording — REGISTER TRANSLATION.** She
   poses for his phone mid-act; the performance is *for* the
   witness and she knows it. In Prima's settled grammar the camera
   is the witness her surrender requires and self-rewatch is her
   conditioning loop — the pose-into-the-lens beats transfer as her
   performing for her own witness. The phone prop and the app
   fiction do not transfer; the address to the lens does.
6. **Tongue as a held segment key — TRANSFER AS-IS.** The tongue is
   held out through a whole segment under a small-amplitude cycle —
   a second tongue usage, distinct from Plan 48's peak-only key:
   tongue as *sustained state* rather than *event*. Both usages now
   on record; a spec chooses per scene.
7. **The internal insert — DOES NOT TRANSFER (imagery); timing
   noted.** A ~9 s dark internal cutaway placed immediately before
   the peak text: an editing rhythm a spec may note without
   importing the device.
8. **Black-cut act structure — TRANSFER AS-IS (editing).** Every
   position change is elided into 1–2 s of black; no transition is
   ever performed. Same elision class as Plan 49's cut descent,
   systematised across a whole piece.
9. **The epilogue register reset — TRANSFER AS-IS (scene-boundary
   technique).** State, costume, light and camera all change at one
   cut, and the audience reads the new register instantly and
   completely. The bundle is the technique: never change the eye
   state alone mid-scene.

## Tempo and camera notes

Greeting block (0–44 s) is pose-held time — beats are 4–14 s holds
separated by single deliberate movements; nothing cycles. The oral
act runs a ≈2.0 s head cycle under long camera holds, and its
37-second face plate (one held extreme close-up; lids, gaze and
blush drift in small increments) removes even the cycle and leaves
drift — the piece's purest state-portrait. The cowgirl act runs the
same ≈2.0 s class; period stretches and amplitude decays together
through the settle — the standard finish shape. The epilogue is
near-static: a slow lean-in over ~13 s. Camera classes map onto
Prima's A-12 set (sub-camera / face-camera family); the epilogue's
third-person wide is the **witness camera** — the camera nobody in
the scene holds, admitted only after the service is over.

## Gaps a Prima spec must author

The induction itself (wholly off-screen backstory — the estate's
authored onset stands unamended); the release (asserted, never
shown — the performed unwind remains authored work); the
standing→kneel descent (she is found kneeling at frame one);
position transitions (all elided); the partner's body grammar
(POV-only until the epilogue); a natural-eye baseline in-register
(the un-glowed eyes appear only in the epilogue's different
register — a target look for the OFF state, not a "before").

## Register discipline

The source register is maledom with a non-consent premise (a
stranger who lets herself in; a hypnosis applied to her in
backstory). The mechanics above transfer; the premise and the
operator voice do not. In Prima's grammar she is in his doorway
because she wants to be, the residue is hers, and the readout — if
one is used — speaks in her voice. Nothing in the transfer set
depends on the source's framing: every mechanism survives the move,
which is the test the prior dossiers applied.
