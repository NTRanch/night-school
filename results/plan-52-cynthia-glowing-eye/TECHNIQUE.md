## The mechanism — what "hypnosis" consists of here

1. **The device.** A glowing cyan eye suspended above and behind
   her head, in frame for the entire clip. Measured: device-crop
   brightness is flat across the clip (YAVG 92.4–99.7 at 4 fps,
   ±3%, no periodicity) — it does not pulse, flash, brighten, or
   activate. The induction is presented as **already complete**
   before frame 1; the device is a standing cause-signifier, not
   an event.
2. **Sightline geometry.** The device hangs on the wall *behind*
   her; her gaze is directed back at the **camera**, never at the
   device. The eye that does the looking in this piece is the
   device's — it watches her, and the POV grammar makes the viewer
   its counterpart. She performs for two watchers: the camera and
   the eye.
3. **Her sustained look-back is the state's proof.** No gaze breaks
   in 5.2 s. Paired with the cause standing in frame, the compliance
   reads without any exposition — the same legibility economy Plan
   49 proved from the other side (eye state readable at full blur),
   here achieved by staging instead of by her eyes.

Set against the programme: Plan 48 puts the signifier **in her
eyes** (hue ramp), Plan 49 **in her eyes** (pattern swap), Plan 50
**on her visor** (display programme). This piece puts it **in the
room**. It is the weakest mechanism of the four as animation
grammar — a set-dressing signifier with no timed behaviour at all —
and its one portable idea is the staging economy itself.

**Her eyes never change state.** Natural blue irises throughout: no
hue shift, no emissive ramp, no pattern swap. The entire eye-state
delta is lid aperture plus a focus *read* — and even that is drift
(below). The clip does not loop (first-vs-last PSNR 15.3 dB) and has
no opening or closing beat.

## Authored vs generation artifact — the honest split

**Authored-looking** (stable across all 21 sheet tiles and 10
full-res frames; read as intentional choices): the staging
(bent-over-desk composition, look-back address, device placement in
the upper frame, sunlight/shadow design, costume fidelity); the
state concept (post-induction, already compliant, performing for
the watcher(s)).

**Generation artifacts** (do not read as beats): **expression
drift** — the lid/focus softening from the engagement peak (≈t1.0,
eyes widest, gaze most direct) to the settled read is gradual,
directionally inconsistent at fine sampling, and unaccompanied by
any brow or mouth event: interpolation drift, usable as a *target
state pair*, not as a transition design. **Global temporal
shimmer** — the motion-energy floor sits at ≈3–5 for the whole
frame for the entire clip; skin sheen, dress highlights and hair
edges crawl every frame. **Contact deformation** — the gripping
hand's fingers and the flesh response vary in plausibility frame
to frame. **Device pupil micro-variation** — brightness measured
flat; the wobble is drift, not a pulse. **Endpoints** — neither is
a designed beat; the clip is a window cut from a longer generated
take. The stroke cycle's period is only **bounded** (≈1.5 s, from
the sheet): the motion-energy series does not resolve a clean
period out of the shimmer floor, and an unresolvable number is
named as such, not sharpened.

## What transfers

- **Transfers as-is — the continuous-gaze mode.** Her look-back is
  *sustained*, not timed: `gaze_target = camera` held for the full
  scene with zero breaks — the direct contrast case to Plan 49's
  phase-locked gaze (contact at stroke tops only). Both read as
  trance; a scene spec chooses the mode per scene. This piece is
  the set's evidence that the continuous mode works when a
  cause-signifier stands in frame.
- **Register translation — the watcher-eye as staging slot.** A
  persistent glowing eye-object in frame establishes "the state is
  active" with no change to her eyes and no exposition. The
  compulsion semantics do not transfer — in the source the eye is
  the master's, watching her. What crosses is the *slot*: one
  persistent visual cause in frame + her sustained address = the
  state reads. Prima's grammar fills the slot with her own devices
  (Plan 50's display programme re-voiced as hers is the same slot
  with the agency inverted).
- **Register translation — the state pair as target keys.**
  Open/direct → heavy-lidded/soft, gaze held on camera throughout,
  is a legitimate two-key expression target for a settled state.
  The *transition* must be authored from the set's real mechanisms
  (Plan 48's eyelid ladder; the consolidation type) — the source's
  drift is not a transition.
- **Translation-with-addition — everything temporal.** This source
  has no onset, no transition, and no release. Any Prima scene
  borrowing its staging imports its induction from Plans 48 / 49 /
  50 and its release from the residue programme's authored work.
  The piece contributes a middle with no ends.

## Does not transfer

- **The mind-control semantics entire.** An external watcher
  imposing the state is the source's fiction; Prima's register is
  her wanting. The device-as-master's-eye is exactly what does not
  cross.
- **The device as a mechanism.** It has no behaviour — no pulse, no
  activation, no coupling to her state. As a Torogao system it
  would be a prop with an emissive material, nothing more; the
  `iris_state` machine remains the estate's induction system, and
  this source adds nothing to it.
- **The artifacts.** Shimmer, contact mush, drift, wobble — named
  above so they are never authored.
