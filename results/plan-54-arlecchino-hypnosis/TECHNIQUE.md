## Technique distillation (mechanisms)

1. **Composed establishing shot in canon costume.** The piece opens
   clothed, in the character's full canon outfit, on a theatrical
   set (coffin + moon backdrop): identity and production value are
   established before any state change. Estate form: state-change
   pieces earn their later registers by spending their first beat
   on the composed baseline.
2. **Eye-state signifier.** The hypnosis state is carried by a
   single readable channel: iris colour/glow shifts to a hot
   pink-magenta with a soft bloom, visible at every framing
   distance used. One channel, no UI, no text — the state is
   legible in a thumbnail. (Compare the estate's own face-state
   discipline: a state that needs a caption has failed.)
3. **Picture-in-picture reaction inset.** At t≈18 s the frame
   carries a bordered inset of the character's face over the wide
   shot — reaction and action in one frame, no cutaway. A
   compositing device for holding the expression register (the
   piece's actual subject) inside body-dominant shots.
4. **Diegetic camera prop.** A video camera sits in-frame on the
   ground in the pond-path scene (t≈33 s): the exhibition framing
   is motivated inside the fiction, and the prop's presence
   re-reads the POV as recorded-performance rather than voyeurism.
5. **Night-exterior lighting recipe.** Moon key (cool, high), warm
   practical accents, and wet/specular ground planes in two of
   three solo sets — reflections double the light field and keep
   night scenes from going flat. Skin carries a persistent
   gloss/specular layer (the "wet look" is constant, not an
   event).
6. **Vignette-chain pacing.** Each location gets exactly one idea
   (set-piece pose → solo display → prop play → partnered close);
   cuts are hard and unmasked. The escalation across the chain is
   carried by scene order and partner entry, not by any single
   scene's internal arc.

## Estate routing

The hypnosis-state signifier (item 2) and the PiP reaction inset
(item 3) are the two portable devices: both speak to Prima's
hypnosis/corruption scene grammar (state legibility; holding the
face register inside wide shots). Items 1, 4, 5, 6 are competent
standard practice, recorded here as confirmation rather than new
doctrine. Item 2 is also the comparison case for Plan 48's
two-step signifier: the binary flip here is what priced the
intermediate hue's readable *falling* phase there. No assets,
models, motion, voice, or text extracted or reused.
