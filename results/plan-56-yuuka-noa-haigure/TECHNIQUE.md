## Progression grammar (the D-1398 study object)

The piece is a **pose-performance escalation**, not a narrative
scene. Its grammar, in order:

1. **Duet establishment (0–~15 s).** Both performers framed
   together in costume; composed, symmetrical posing; the
   audience-facing "act" register. Expressions composed/smug.
2. **Solo feature rotation (~15–55 s).** Alternating solo
   close-ups (face-dominant framing) and full-body pose holds;
   water/spray effects enter as a spectacle layer over the
   costumes (wet-look gloss is a state marker: the piece reads
   "later" when the gloss is on). Partner reactions are cut in as
   punctuation (the laughing close-up at ~33 s).
3. **Teasing-superiority exchange (~55–90 s).** The expression
   register shifts from composed to provocative: direct-to-camera
   smug close-up (t≈66 s, hands at chin — a held "your move"
   beat), reclined/inverted framings, body-dominant crops. The
   on-screen chant text appears as a graphic chorus — the piece's
   only text device, used as rhythm, not dialogue.
4. **Shared abandon close (~90–140 s).** Framing tightens and
   inverts (upside-down pair shots), expressions open fully
   (open-mouth laughter in the t≈130 s two-shot), and the cut rate
   peaks. Both performers end in the same register — mutual,
   playful, unguarded.

**The transferable mechanism (D-1398's question):** desire is
staged as a *performance the performers progressively enjoy
giving*. The escalation dial is not exposure — costume state is
nearly constant — it is **expression register × framing intimacy
× edit tempo**: composed → teasing → abandoned, carried by
(1) face-first close-ups before body crops, (2) the partner's
reaction shots as the pacing metronome, (3) a graphic chorus
device marking the refrain, and (4) a wet/gloss state layer that
timestamps "after" without a costume change. Positive traits are
preserved throughout — both characters read as willing performers
from the first frame; there is no reluctance register anywhere in
the 14 sampled frames. This is the same inversion the Prima
corruption-sim programme adopted (desire as the progression
engine), here in its pure performance form.
