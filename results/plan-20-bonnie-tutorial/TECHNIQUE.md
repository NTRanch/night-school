# Plan 20 — Pass 2: Method comparison vs Plan 01

The distillation question, per problem: **which contact
method survives both creators' evidence?** Plan 01's method
is Blender-taught (seated blocking, Child Of locks, sparse
keys + Make Cyclic). The Bonnie series' method is SFM-built
(all-fours blocking, generated FK rig, dense authored keys,
no locks) — its rig layer observed in THIS plan's segment
(OBSERVATION §4), its contact/blocking layer carried by the
series record (Plan 15 C-04/C-05/C-09/C-11/C-13, same
sitting, same rig). Each problem below states both
solutions, the evidence each carries, and the verdict. The
numerical half of the comparison is PASS3-REBUILD.md: both
methods built on the same estate pair and gated with Plan
01's own measures.

## Problem 1 — Contact lock (pelvic registration)

- **Plan 01's solution: solver-held.** A Child Of bone
  constraint (Influence 1.000, Set Inverse after posing)
  re-parents the leader's contact point into the follower's
  Hips frame; the solver maintains registration every frame.
  Estate form: a contact empty + the leader's Hips keys
  SOLVED against the frozen frame-0 offset. Evidence:
  constraint-form drift max **0.0001 mm**; shipped keyed
  form max **0.393 mm** (interpolation residual between
  solve keys) — measured, PASS3 record of Plan 01.
- **Bonnie solution: authored.** No constraint exists
  anywhere in the series' record (this plan's C-09 for the
  rig phase; Plan 15 C-12 for the posing phase). The cycle
  is dense baked keys on rig_pelvis/rig_hips; registration
  is whatever the keys hold, verified continuously in the
  shot camera while posing. Evidence: the method WORKS in
  the source (the finished animation reads registered from
  a camera riding the action — Plan 15's rebuild measured
  the POV consequences), but the source carries NO drift
  number: its verification is the eye, in-camera, at
  authoring time.
- **Estate rebuild evidence (this plan, PASS3 §a):** the
  authored method transplanted to the estate pair, measured
  on Plan 01's terms, drifts **max 9.85 mm / mean 3.97 mm**
  at the contact — 25× the keyed-solve class, ~10⁵× the
  constraint class. Its hands (Problem 3) drift further.
- **VERDICT: Plan 01's method survives — solver-held
  registration is the estate default.** The authored method
  is not refuted as craft (it ships finished work in its
  own tool, where the author verifies in-camera
  continuously); it is refuted as an ESTATE method, because
  the estate's clips are consumed by other scenes, girls,
  and cameras than the authoring one — a registration that
  only the author's eye verified does not transfer, and
  9.85 mm is the measured cost of transferring it anyway.

## Problem 2 — Phase / reaction design

- **Plan 01's solution: leader/follower grammar.** Sparse
  keys (leader 0/15/31, follower denser ~6/12/17/20/26/31),
  hold-until-Contact, reaction peak pulled −2 frames early,
  feet last-and-subtlest, breast lag hand-keyed. Evidence:
  built and measured — follower Spine holds 10.0° at
  Contact, peaks 14.0° at +7 samples, endpoints the same
  mid-recovery pose (Plan 01 PASS3 §2).
- **Bonnie solution: counter-phase dense keying.** Select
  key parts first, bookmark the loop length, pose the
  middle, then key pelvis and hips in COUNTER-PHASE
  ("as the pelvis moves down the hips move up" — Plan 15
  C-05, speech + graph-editor corroboration); reaction is
  not a separate grammar — it is inside the dense field,
  shaped by SFM's procedural curve operators
  (Smooth/Jitter/Stagger/Hold) and the head's aim blend.
  Evidence: session-level (the dense counter-phased
  channels are screen-visible; amplitudes UNREADABLE —
  Plan 15 U-2).
- **Estate rebuild evidence:** the method build kept Plan
  01's phase TABLES (the comparison isolates the contact
  method, not the grammar) and its follower still holds
  until Contact and peaks at +7 (Spine 22.0° → 26.0° at
  sample 45) — i.e. the grammar is method-independent.
  Separately, Plan 19's 60 s protocol composes Plan 01's
  grammar with D-1072's C2 follow-lag; this plan's
  execution measured what that composition actually does
  (PASS3 §b: the C2 spring adds a SECOND +8-sample lag on
  top of the authored +7 — a finding about composition,
  recorded with numbers).
- **VERDICT: HYBRID, named.** Leader/follower phase grammar
  (Plan 01) is the estate's reaction design — it is the
  only one of the two that exists as checkable rules. From
  the Bonnie side, adopt the **counter-phase keying
  discipline for the leader's own cycle** (pelvis/hips
  counter-phase is a stronger authoring rule for the thrust
  channel than Plan 01's rotation-share heuristic alone)
  and the **dense-key substrate** as the estate's clip
  interchange form (the fleet already ships dense-evaluated
  clips; D-1072's machinery assumes it). The procedural
  preset operators themselves are REJECTED (tool-bound —
  Plan 15's sheet already rules this).

## Problem 3 — Hand registration

- **Plan 01's solution: Child Of to the closest thigh
  deform bone** (proximity principle: minimise the lever
  arm between lock and surface), estate form = solved keys.
  Evidence: frame-0 residuals ≤0.46 mm follower / ≤0.39 mm
  leader; across-loop dev max 0.49 mm (her hands) / 4.94 mm
  (his, interpolation between her keyframes).
- **Bonnie solution: posed, finger-by-finger, for the
  frame.** Hands are planted and posed at finger level,
  staged for what the shot camera will see (Plan 15 C-10);
  no lock. In this plan's segment the hands' rig half is
  visible: "rig fingers" ON in the Auto-Rigger — finger
  control exists because the method NEEDS finger-level
  posing as its registration mechanism.
- **Estate rebuild evidence:** authored hands on the estate
  pair deviate **max 49.0 mm** (follower's floor plants,
  riding her spine reaction) and **max 33.2 mm** (leader's
  grip offsets) across the loop — one to two orders of
  magnitude beyond the solved form, on identical measures.
- **VERDICT: Plan 01's method survives — solved keys to the
  closest-bone target is the estate default.** Bonnie's
  finger-by-finger posing survives as a NECESSARY
  COMPLEMENT, not an alternative: Plan 01's own build poses
  the grip before solving it (the lock preserves a posed
  grip; it does not create one — T-11's invariant). The
  hybrid is already the estate's practice; this comparison
  confirms it with numbers from both sides.

## Problem 4 — Penetration handling

- **Plan 01's solution: measure + sculpt.** The constraint
  prevents drift, not penetration (Plan 01 binding finding
  3); residual interpenetration is measured on the contact
  set CAMERA-INDEPENDENTLY and cured by a named corrective
  shape key sculpted in-pose at 1.0, never on Basis.
  Evidence: 5.97 mm pre-cure → **5.46 mm** post-cure max on
  the built loop; the creator's camera-favoured tolerance
  explicitly does NOT transfer.
- **Bonnie solution: pose + favour the camera.** Contact is
  posed with the work camera behind the partner at close
  range (Plan 15 §5); the shot camera's sightline is the
  arbiter, and the all-fours blocking keeps the contact set
  geometrically open to that sightline (Plan 15's framing
  finding). No penetration measurement exists in the
  series' record — the artefact carries none to adopt.
- **Estate rebuild evidence:** in the all-fours arrangement
  on the estate pair, the same two-axis test finds **0.0 mm
  at every sample** (frame-0 gap tuned to 3.0 mm; the
  authored advance never closes it into overlap) — the
  BLOCKING carries a clearance the seated arrangement does
  not (Plan 01's seated build grazed 5.97 mm with the same
  phase tables). The cure procedure was stood ready and not
  invoked: there was nothing to cure.
- **VERDICT: HYBRID, named.** Plan 01's measure-and-cure
  discipline is the estate gate (a number or it didn't
  happen); the Bonnie BLOCKING is adopted as a clearance
  strategy — where a scene's act permits all-fours-class
  geometry, arrange for clearance by construction first,
  then measure, then cure what remains. Blocking-as-
  clearance is the one penetration idea the Bonnie record
  genuinely adds, and the rebuild's 0.0 mm is its evidence.

## Problem 5 — Loop closure

- **Plan 01's solution: identical endpoints + cyclic
  channels.** Keys 0/15/31, Make Cyclic over exactly the
  full span, follower endpoints a mid-recovery pose (the
  seam law + T-08 construction). Evidence: seam deltas
  **0.000 mm / 0.000°** over 108 bones; seam frames
  pixel-identical; repetition identical over 3 cycles.
- **Bonnie solution: dense cycle + bookmarked length.**
  The loop length is set first (~0.8 s shot; the cycle
  keyed dense across it); closure is a property of the
  dense field's repetition, checked by watching the shot
  camera. Evidence: session-level; no seam number exists
  in the source record.
- **Estate rebuild evidence:** the dense-authored build's
  seam is ALSO exact (0.000 mm / 0.000°, pixel-identical
  endpoints) — because its phase tables are periodic by
  construction, density is irrelevant to closure. What
  differs is VERIFIABILITY: Plan 01's construction makes
  the seam a theorem of the clip; the dense field makes it
  an accident of the field that must be re-measured per
  clip (this plan measured it; the source never could).
- **VERDICT: Plan 01's method survives — seam-by-
  construction (identical endpoint keys, cyclic over the
  full span, mid-recovery follower endpoints) is the
  estate law.** Density is adopted as interchange form
  (Problem 2) but closure is never left to it.
