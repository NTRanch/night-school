## Application — NSFW + SFW

The grammar is **rating-blind**: every item is a statement
about frame fractions, order of operations, and output
form — nothing in G1–G12 references content rating, and
the audit proved the point operationally: the SFW and NSFW
variants of the Plan 02 template scored IDENTICALLY on
every item (fractions agree ≤0.006). What transfers:
- **NSFW lanes (Strobe/Torogao stills):** adopt the bands
  (G3/G4/G6) as the stills-lane framing gate; adopt G9's
  invariant (face finished last, against the frame); G10
  licenses single-angle pose construction for stills
  deliverables; G12 as the draft economy.
- **SFW lanes (key art, store art, marketing stills):** the
  SAME bands apply as a review instrument — the audit method
  (ground the figure/head, convert to fractions, score per
  item) joins SFW key-art review unchanged; a loose,
  high-headroom key-art frame is now a measurable finding
  (fill/headroom/face-y numbers), not a vibe. G1's rule
  (crop to the interesting parts) applies to key art
  composition even where the delivery size is fixed —
  the crop is chosen by framing, the size by the platform.
- **What does NOT transfer:** his worked example's content,
  model, and outfit specifics (one commercial model's UI);
  the 77 mm number as anything but a per-series choice;
  resolution retuning as a workflow step.
