## Lesson (shelf placement is not evidence)

A "Tutorials" category placement on rule34video carries **no
presumption that the item teaches anything**. The Night
School programme now holds three named instances, each
established from the item's own captured record, not from
its title:

- **Plan 03** (3300899): re-scoped at intake — a Virt-A-Mate
  editor pattern-test clip, not a tutorial.
- **Plan 35** (3352570): premise refuted by the screen — a
  behind-the-scenes posing recording set to music, zero
  named defects, zero narration.
- **Plan 14** (4293878): pure content — two narrative
  episodes of a subscription serial; 0 of 4 tutorial markers
  (UI, instruction text, to-viewer narration, process
  demonstration) in 61 structurally sampled frames; every
  page tag a content tag.

**Transferable rule:** any curriculum or intake built from a
shelf listing treats placement and title as *unverified
claims*; title-ambiguous items get a triage (page record +
sparse structural sampling) before any full study is
budgeted. Plan 14's triage closed a five-pass plan for the
cost of a page fetch, a 420 MB capture, and 61 frames — the
proportionate instrument for a shelf this unreliable.
