## What enters estate render practice

**Both lanes (SFW shared with NSFW):**

1. **Light-the-subject-alone as a build step.** Append set and
   character as separate collections; zero the world; light the
   subject from silhouette; reunite. This is also the structural
   vaccine against the Plan 11 dead-rim class: lamps placed against
   black, one at a time, cannot silently die behind set geometry.
   (Evidence: OBSERVATION §3.1/§3.4; PASS3 §3 rim 0.000663 vs 0.0.)
2. **Light Path → Transparent BSDF** for set geometry that eats
   interior light — a named fix in the lumen/torogao toolbox
   (OBSERVATION §3.5).
3. **The render-pair principle, restated:** the noise *threshold*
   (0.0100) is now a two-voice constant (02, 40); the *ceiling* is
   negotiable against a named downstream denoise stage. Estate
   keeper stills keep the current pair; drafts may run 128-class
   ceilings when a second denoise is budgeted (TECHNIQUE T-40-7/9).
4. **The grading contract as a compositor chain** (TECHNIQUE §3):
   FilmicBlend → TealOrange → Contrast → Glow → Vignette → Sharpen,
   built and measured here as the NS40 grade. Proposed as a *named
   reusable comp preset* for stills that want the dusk read — not as
   a replacement for the NS02 look.

**NSFW keeper stills lane:**

5. **NS40-ResolveFirst as a standing variant** alongside NS02 for
   interior/dusk scenes: distributed micro-rig (no lamp > 25.7 W
   class), character/set separability, dark plate + grade finish.
   It is the better tool when the scene is a dark interior with
   practicals; NS02 remains the tool for the neon-backdrop read
   (PASS3 difference 5 — the variant is set-dependent).
6. **Real DoF (f/2.4 class)** as a sanctioned finish for keeper
   stills where the template's deep focus reads synthetic
   (OBSERVATION §3.5; untested in the rebuild by design, C-40-6).

**Not proposed:** DWAA for estate EXR interchange (Plan 11 ZIP ruling
stands); an external Resolve dependency in the estate pipeline (the
contract is host-portable and now exists in compositor form);
kilowatt-key retirement (refuted as *necessity*, not as practice —
TECHNIQUE §5).

## Updated triangulation summary (for the coordinator)

Four voices now stand on the lighting question. **Where the look is
finished** has three positions: in the render (02, 17 with a global
trim), in the compositor from passes (11/13), and **outside Blender
entirely** (40, Resolve) — the estate's own practice spans the first
two; 40 proves the third is a coherent professional route, and its
contract ports back into the compositor (PASS3 §2). **The 0.0100
threshold is the corpus's first two-voice render constant**;
ceilings are per-creator (4096 / 512 / 128) and track how much
downstream denoise each voice budgets. **Lamp scale is now a
measured spectrum**, not a doctrine: 1000 W key (02, 75.6% share) →
50 W fills (17) → ≤25.7 W distributed field (40, no source >27.1%),
and the delivered looks differ in shadow structure exactly as the
shares predict. **The dead rim remains the corpus's standing
caution**: 02's rim measures 0.0 again in this run's fresh copy;
40's construction avoids the geometry structurally while its own
rim measures only 0.000663 — occlusion-freedom and rim *strength*
are separate variables, and only per-group measurement tells them
apart. Full rows: TECHNIQUE §4 (AGREE 2 · CONFLICT 6 · SINGLE 4 ·
DIFFERENT-PHILOSOPHY 1, with two split rows counted once each).
