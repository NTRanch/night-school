## The display state machine (the headline device)

This source's induction signifier is not on the body at all — it is
a **worn display running a text programme**, and it specs cleanly:

- `spiral_layer`: continuous rotation, magenta/violet, constant
  across every state — the carrier wave. Only its emissive level
  changes (bright as content, dim as worn furniture once the panel
  is revealed as a headset).
- `primary_text`: one word/phrase at a time, large, white, holds of
  **≈1–3 s** ("Obey" longest at ≈3 s; most ≈1–2 s). The programme's
  sequence grammar is itself data: **command → label → praise →
  state assertion → instruction** (Obey → Hypno slut → Good girl →
  You are so turned on → Touch yourself).
- `ghost_text[]`: a second channel — small, low-opacity words
  crossfading (≈0.5 s) under and around the primary, including
  state words and one direct address ("keep watching"). Two
  channels at two opacities is the whole trick: a legible layer and
  a subliminal layer, independently keyable.
- **Transfers as-is** as a state machine. The *words* are this
  source's register (see flags); the two-layer text mechanism, the
  hold timings, and the sequence grammar are register-free.

## Occlusion-reveal: the third eye-state device in the set

The hypnosis sources now disagree three ways, and the disagreement
is the yield:

- Plan 48: the eye change is a **visible ramp** (hue steps watched
  in close-up).
- Plan 49: the change is a **visible consolidation** (flash, decay,
  pattern resolving in view).
- This plan: the change is **occluded** — the panel covers the face
  for the entire transition (≈4.25 s) and the reveal *is* the
  proof: natural blue at t2, magenta spiral-patterned and emissive
  at t6.75, lids ≈⅓ lowered, gaze down and unfocused — no in-between
  frame exists.

A scene spec should treat these as three interchangeable transition
types for the same state machine (`iris_state` natural → trance):
`ramp`, `consolidation`, `occlusion_reveal` — picked per scene, not
per doctrine. Occlusion-reveal is the cheapest to author (two keys,
no ramp) and the most device-dependent (it needs an occluder in the
fiction).

## Supporting devices

- **Glyph contact markers.** Glowing glyphs sit at the wrist cuffs,
  on the glove, on the chest emblem, and — in the finale —
  projected at the two touch contact points. Read mechanically,
  they are **contact indicators**: an emissive marker bound to each
  active hand target, on at contact, off on release. Transfers
  as-is as a staging device.
- **The counter as state meter.** The finale's escalation is
  carried numerically (arousal 40.1 → 78.6 → 98.2 → ≈100, clock
  running at exactly real-time). A visible state value the audience
  can read the arc off: a spec exposes `state_value` and lets
  presentation render it; the source's HUD is one rendering.
- **The telegraphed peak.** A 1 Hz verbal countdown ("Orgasm in
  3·2·1·now") for the last ≈3 s converts climax from an event into
  an appointment — anticipation authored at 1 s intervals, ≈3 s
  out. The timing shape transfers; who does the counting changes
  (see flags).
- **Viewer-address arc.** The full-screen cards are diegetically
  aimed at her eyes and compositionally aimed at the audience's —
  the viewer is shown the stimulus itself. The finale then re-seats
  the viewer as the operator (surveillance frame, subject name,
  program status). Address arc: subject → operator, done with
  camera/HUD grammar alone.

## Camera grammar — the mapping to Prima's broadcast set

Her-POV down-body → a **sub-camera insert** (her own view of
herself; the self-rewatch grammar rendered live). Frontal witness
close-up → the **face camera**, with the display panel as an
in-fiction prop between her and it. Monitor wide → the **main
camera**, HUD and all. The full-screen cards are the fourth
position: **the camera inside the display** — the audience shown
her stimulus rather than her reaction; in a Prima scene, a
sub-camera pointed at what she is watching.

## Gaps — what the spec must author

1. **Any performed onset.** The face is occluded for the whole
   transition and the body is seated and still; there is no falling
   phase — no posture change, no tremor, no softening beat anywhere
   in the 24.2 s. A scene wanting a *performed* onset composites
   from Plans 48 / 49; this source contributes the display
   programme and the reveal, not the fall.
2. **Standing / full-body vocabulary: none.** Seated or reclined in
   every frame.
3. **The after-face.** The headset never comes off; the eyes are
   never shown again after the reveal. A spec needing the
   post-state face authors it from the reveal's parameters.
4. **Any release / aftermath.** The file ends mid-decay (counter at
   87.6 and falling, hands still at stations). Import the stages,
   never the ending shape.

## Register flags

- **Transfers as-is:** the two-layer display state machine;
  occlusion-reveal as a transition type; glyph contact markers; the
  readable state value; the countdown's timing shape; the finale's
  two-station hand choreography; the camera-switching grammar.
- **Register translations:** the **display's voice** — in the source
  the words are an operator's imperatives imposed through a device
  she did not choose; in Prima's grammar the display is *hers*: the
  primary word is what she is asking for, the ghost layer what she
  is admitting. The **countdown** — an operator counting a subject
  down becomes her own anticipation made legible. The **monitor
  frame** — surveillance of a subject becomes her broadcast's
  main-camera overlay, which she directs.
- **Does not transfer:** the program fiction itself
  ("obedience.vr", "Subject name:", iteration counters) — the
  clinical operator register is the source's compulsion semantics
  wearing a HUD; the degrading label words as *content* (the
  ghost-layer *mechanism* transfers, its vocabulary is
  re-authored); and the premise that the device overrides her.
