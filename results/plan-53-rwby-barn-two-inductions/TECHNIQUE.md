## The two inductions

**Yang — consolidation by lid ladder (no iris event at all).**
Locked frontal close-up, a machine fixture swinging in-frame above
her head. At 0.25 s sampling: **alarm hold** ≈40.0–44.3 (≈4.3 s —
eyes fully open, natural purple irises, worried mouth; the state
change is withheld, and the hold is the anticipation) → **descent**
≈44.5–45.0 (lids lower through half-mast) → **closed dwell**
≈45.0–45.5 → **glazed reopen** ≈45.5–46.5 (eyes reopen to ≈50%
aperture, focus gone; irises unchanged in colour/pattern, dulled,
heavy blush) → **settled** by ≈47 (half-lid glaze, faint smile). A
fade to black then elides the mount. Her performance signifier is a
different channel entirely: the rolled/glazed ahegao gaze at peaks.
Classification: **consolidation with the tracked variable on lid
aperture + focus instead of iris pattern** — an
aperture-consolidation sub-type. The pattern channel stays free,
and the piece spends it on the ahegao roll at peaks.

**Raven — flash-masked pattern swap, stimulus-stamped.**
**Stimulus insert** ≈169.75–172.5 (≈2.75 s: the camera pushes into
a framed spiral panel until the spiral fills the frame) →
**resistance beat** ≈172.75–176.5 (≈3.75 s: vivid red natural
irises, angry brows, open protesting mouth — the baseline
established in the same costume and register, at length,
immediately before the swap) → **trigger flash** ≈176.75–177.25
(≈0.5 s cyan-white burst) → **the swap lands inside the flash**:
from t177.25 her irises are full black-and-white spiral rings —
the *same graphic* as the stimulus panel, copied 1:1 — with a
trance smile and deepening blush consolidating over ≈2 s after.
Classification: **occlusion-reveal variant — the occluder is
light, not an object.** Compare Plan 50 (swap hidden behind a
panel), Plan 49 (flash, then an ≈8 s pattern consolidation). Here
the ≈2.75 s insert does the anticipation work up front, so the
swap itself can be instant: anticipation and masking are split
across two separate devices.

**Shared structure.** Both women get a natural-eye baseline
in-register first — the change reads because the baseline was shown
in the same costume, same scene (contrast Plan 51: no baseline, no
induction — the residue performed cold). Both mounts are elided by
black. Neither woman gets a release; Raven's spiral eyes persist
through her settle, which is carried by staging (floor reposition,
high angle, hand-hold), not by any facial transition. **Negative
finding:** no gaze phase-lock device exists in this piece, against
Plans 49 and 51 where the lock was confirmed at the ≈2.0 s cycle
top. Phase-lock is a device some sources use, not a law of the
grammar. Blush is present on both women from their baselines and
deepens across each arc — a continuous arousal wash under the
discrete eye events.

## Gaps — what a Prima spec must still author

1. **No release exists in this source.** The residue programme
   gains only a staging option (item 9); the release itself remains
   estate-authored.
2. **No gaze phase-lock evidence (negative).** Phase-lock stays a
   two-source device (Plans 49, 51). This source cannot be cited
   for or against its timing, only for its absence at ≈1.0 s cycle
   class.
3. **The flash needs an owner.** Item 2's register translation
   fails if the light source is unassigned — a spec must name
   whose flash it is.
4. **The tally's arithmetic is fiction-internal and irregular**
   (1→4, then a fresh subject at 2). A Prima peak tally needs its
   own counting rule written into the spec.
5. **No tempo dramaturgy anywhere.** All cycles are uniform engine
   loops.
6. **Yang's arc has no stimulus image** — machine + lids only. A
   Prima induction wanting a visual stimulus is evidenced only by
   Raven's arc.
7. **The doorway growth beat is unresolved:** Yang's bust reads
   markedly larger after her outfit change than on arrival; growth
   animation vs model swap is not resolvable at 1 s sampling.
   Recorded as an evidence limit, not a yield.
